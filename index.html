The VA Loan Advantage

A benefit earned through service.
Built to be used.

The VA home loan isn't a shortcut — it's a congressionally mandated reward for military service. Here's what makes it the most powerful mortgage program available.

⬛

Zero Down Payment

Qualified veterans can purchase a home with no down payment — on any loan amount. No minimums, no tricks.

◼

No PMI. Ever.

Conventional loans charge Private Mortgage Insurance until you hit 20% equity. VA loans never do — saving you hundreds every month.

▪

Competitive Rates

VA loans consistently offer rates at or below conventional loan averages — because the VA backing reduces lender risk.

◾

Reusable for Life

Your VA benefit doesn't expire and doesn't "run out." Use it once, twice, or five times — it's yours for life.

▫

No Loan Limits*

As of 2020, veterans with full entitlement have no VA loan limit — not in Fresno, not in Los Angeles, not anywhere in California.

▪

Disability Exemption

Veterans with a 10%+ service-connected disability rating pay zero VA Funding Fee — saving thousands at closing.

10 lies that are keeping

"I need to save a down payment before I can buy."

The Truth

The VA loan requires zero down payment. No minimums. No exceptions. This is a congressionally mandated benefit — not a loophole.

02

"Sellers won't accept VA offers. They take too long."

Tap to reveal →
The Truth

VA loans close in 40–50 days — same as conventional. The issue is lender selection, not the loan. The right VA lender wins deals.

03

"I already used my VA loan. It's a one-time thing."

Tap to reveal →
The Truth

VA loan entitlement is reusable for life. Veterans have used it 3, 4, even 5 times. Sell, restore, repeat.

04

"The VA Funding Fee wipes out all the savings."

Tap to reveal →
The Truth

The funding fee is one time. PMI on a conventional loan charges you every year. After 5 years, VA buyers are thousands ahead — and disabled vets pay $0.

05

"VA loan limits are too low for California prices."

Tap to reveal →
The Truth

VA loan limits were eliminated in 2020. No cap. No ceiling. If you qualify for the payment, there's no limit on the price.

06

"I'm National Guard. That's not the same — I don't qualify."

Tap to reveal →
The Truth

Guard and Reserve members do qualify — after 6 years of service or 90+ days on Title 10 federal orders. Your service counts.

07

"My husband passed. His VA benefits died with him."

Tap to reveal →
The Truth

Surviving spouses of veterans who died in service or from a service-connected disability may be fully eligible — in their own name.

08

"My credit score is too low to get approved."

Tap to reveal →
The Truth

The VA sets no minimum credit score. VA underwriters look at your full story — not just a number. Many lenders work with scores as low as 580.

09

"VA inspection requirements kill deals with sellers."

Tap to reveal →
The Truth

VA Minimum Property Requirements protect you, not the lender. Move-in ready homes pass with no issues. These requirements exist for your benefit.

10

"VA loans are only for first-time homebuyers."

Tap to reveal →
The Truth

The VA loan has no first-time buyer requirement. It doesn't matter how many homes you've bought before. It only cares that you served.

Eligibility

Who qualifies for
the VA home loan?

More people than you think. Here are the four groups most often left out of the conversation.

🎖️

Veterans

Honorably discharged veterans with 90+ days of active duty service during wartime, or 181+ days during peacetime.

  • Any branch of service
  • Discharge must be other than dishonorable
  • Benefit is reusable for life
⚔️

Active Duty

Currently serving military members are eligible after 90 continuous days of active duty service.

  • All branches including Coast Guard
  • Statement of Service required
  • Can buy near any duty station
Often Overlooked
🛡️

Guard & Reserve

National Guard and Reserve members qualify — and most don't know it.

  • 6 years of service with honorable discharge
  • OR 90+ days on Title 10 federal orders
  • Post-9/11 deployments create additional paths
Often Overlooked
⭐

Surviving Spouses

Unremarried surviving spouses of veterans who died in service or from a service-connected disability.

  • Fully eligible in their own name
  • Zero down payment access
  • Some remarried spouses may still qualify
