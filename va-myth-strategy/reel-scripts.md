## SCRIPT 1 — "Myth vs. Truth" Fast-Cut Talking Head Format
**Myth Addressed:** Myth #1 — "You need a down payment to buy with a VA loan"
**Target Length:** 60–75 seconds

---

### HOOK — First 3 Seconds

**Spoken:**
"Someone told you to save up before you buy. They gave you the wrong advice."

*[Visual direction: Host speaks directly to camera, close-up, confident. No intro. No "hey guys." Cut straight to the line.]*

**On-Screen Text:**
[THEY LIED TO YOU.]

---

### BODY — Myth Setup → Truth Reveal → So What?

**Spoken:**
"Here's the myth:
To use the VA loan, you need a down payment.
Maybe 3%. Maybe 5%. Maybe 10%.

That is completely false.

[CUT — slight jump cut]

The VA home loan was designed — by Congress — to allow qualified veterans to buy a home with zero down.

Not 1%.
Not 'just a little something.'
Zero.

[CUT]

I'm talking about a $400,000 home.
A $600,000 home.
With no down payment.

Because you already paid for it.
You paid for it in years.
In deployments.
In sacrifice.

[CUT]

Now here's why this myth keeps spreading.

Conventional loans require a down payment.
FHA requires a down payment.
Most people assume all loans work the same way.

They don't.

The VA loan has its own rules.
And one of the biggest rules is: you don't need to bring cash to closing.

[CUT — host leans slightly forward]

So if someone — a family member, a real estate agent, a lender — told you to 'get your money together first' before buying…

They were thinking about the wrong loan."

## Production Notes (All Scripts)

**Pacing:** Scripts 1 and 3 = fast-cut energy. Script 2 = intentional pauses — silence is part of the storytelling.

**Captions:** All Reels should have full auto-captions enabled. Review and correct before posting — VA terminology often gets garbled by auto-caption AI.

**B-Roll Suggestions (if available):**
- Script 1: Home exterior, "SOLD" sign, calculator/no-down-payment graphic
- Script 2: Quiet home interior (warm, personal feeling), VA certificate graphic
- Script 3: Guard/Reserve training imagery (if cleared for use), COE screenshot with PII blurred

**Hook Frame:** The very first frame of the video (before audio begins) should show an eye-catching graphic or text overlay — Instagram often shows this as a thumbnail and it determines whether users swipe past or stop.

**Recommended Thumbnail Text:**
- Script 1: "ZERO DOWN — THE VA LOAN TRUTH"
- Script 2: "SHE DIDN'T KNOW SHE QUALIFIED"
- Script 3: "GUARD & RESERVE: YOU MAY ALREADY QUALIFY"
